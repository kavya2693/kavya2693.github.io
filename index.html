About

I lead AI/ML and data-product work at Bristol Myers Squibb, and before that spent eight years turning messy real-world data into decisions — e-commerce logistics at Noon in Dubai, supply and demand forecasting at Shell, and operations analytics before that.

The thread through all of it is knowledge graphs and Graph-RAG: RDF/OWL, SHACL validation, SPARQL, and graph databases used not as a demo but as production infrastructure. It is a narrow, hard-won niche, and it is where I do my best work — in healthcare and pharma, energy, and retail.

I am based in Dubai on a UAE Golden Visa. The projects below are the public ones; several more live behind enterprise walls.
